How Hunt scores a find

Hunt marks BUY, PASS, or CHECK from a verified sold price. Net is the sold price times 0.87 minus $5.

Hunt is a website first. A find is BUY only when a verified sold price has a source URL and a date, and the net clears the bar. Net is the sold price times 0.87 minus $5. That net has to beat retail times 1.09 by at least $15, and the comp has to be liquid. A net under retail is PASS. Everything else is CHECK, including an ask with no settled sale.
