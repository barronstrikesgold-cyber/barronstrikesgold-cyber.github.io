Why some cards have no photo

Hunt shows a product photo only from a licensed host. Logo images stay off the card.

A product photo appears only when the license file names that host. Logo URLs are not shown. Unlicensed hosts stay as a category placeholder, including on detail pages. The operator is Broken Beaker Labs LC. The same rule covers Hot Wheels, trading cards, sneakers, and designer pieces.
