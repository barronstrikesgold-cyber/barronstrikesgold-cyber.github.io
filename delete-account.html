Delete account

Hunt is operated by Broken Beaker Labs LC. This page is public. You do not need the app, and you do not need an account, to read it.

There is no account yet

This version of Hunt does not offer sign-in. There is no server account to delete, and Broken Beaker Labs LC does not hold a cloud copy of your hunt. Google sign-in and an email magic link are planned. Sign in with Apple comes later, after an Apple Developer account exists.

Delete data on this device

Open Hunt, then My Stuff, then Settings. Choose Delete my data on this device and confirm. That removes every hunt- and reseller- key in this browser, including tracked items, alerts, inventory, settings, coupons, and theme. Hunt then keeps an empty inventory record so the starter book is not copied back. That also clears the IndexedDB databases hunt-stock (inventory tracker, Show Mode sales, item photos) and hunt-upc (learned catalog and barcode cache). Export my data on that same screen downloads a JSON file of all of it first if you want a copy.

When an account exists

Deletion of a future account is planned to remove the sign-in, the profile (email, age confirmation, and email opt-in), and the synced tracked items, inventory, alerts, and settings. A copy that is still on a device stays until you delete it there. The request is planned to finish within 30 days. Backup copies, once a paid database plan is in use, are planned to roll off within 7 days. The in-app control is planned to ask you to confirm before anything is removed.
